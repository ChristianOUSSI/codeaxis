Privacy policy

This page explains, in simple terms, what CODE AXIS Digital Cameroon does with the information you send it. Last update: November 2026.

What information we collect

  • The information you enter in our forms: name, WhatsApp phone, email if you give one, date of birth, neighbourhood or city, situation and motivation for the Youth Partnership application; name, email and message for a simple contact.
  • Nothing else: no advertising cookie, no third-party tracker, no profiling. Our traffic statistics stay within the hosting provider.

Why and on what basis

Your information is used only to process your application or request: calling you back, checking eligibility (under 35 for the Youth Partnership), preparing the interview and, once signed, drawing up the partnership contract. The legal basis is your consent, given through the form checkbox, and the performance of pre-contract talks.

How it travels

The contact form first attempts a direct send through a function hosted on Vercel, which forwards your message by email to CODE AXIS without storing it in a database; if that service is unavailable, your mail app opens. The Youth Partnership application goes by WhatsApp or email, which you confirm yourself on your device. These exchanges pass through Vercel and our email delivery provider, Meta (WhatsApp) or your email provider, then remain in our internal recruitment files.

How long

An unsuccessful application is erased after twelve months. A signed partnership file is kept for the length of the contract plus the legal accounting and tax retention periods in Cameroon.

Your rights

You can ask to view, correct or delete your information, without justification, by writing to contact@codeaxis.cm or on WhatsApp at +237 691 223 916 mentioning "personal data". We answer within thirty days. If you refuse the processing, do not tick the box: the form will not go.
