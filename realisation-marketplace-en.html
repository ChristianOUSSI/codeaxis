E-commerce & growth case study

National B2B & B2C marketplace

Omnichannel sales platform with local payments (Orange Money, MTN MoMo), multi-store management and a dedicated courier mobile app.

Context & problem

A Cameroonian wholesaler wanted to move from a physical business to a full digital presence, letting individuals buy online and B2B resellers place wholesale orders with tiered pricing.

The main challenge: integrating local mobile payments (Orange Money, MTN MoMo), often unavailable in Western e-commerce solutions, while supporting the wholesaler's entire catalogue.

CODE AXIS solution

We built a multi-vendor marketplace with dual client spaces (B2B and B2C), proprietary local payment APIs, and a courier mobile app managing routes and delivery confirmations in real time.

The multi-warehouse stock system automatically synchronises availability between the online store and physical points of sale.

Technology stack

Next.js 14 Node.js + NestJS MongoDB React Native Orange Money API MTN MoMo API Redis AWS S3 + CloudFront

Project timeline

  • Phase 1: UX workshops with the client and its key resellers, benchmarking of local solutions
  • Phase 2: High-fidelity Figma mockups and client approval
  • Phase 3: B2B/B2C web platform development and local payment integration
  • Phase 4: Delivery driver mobile app and logistics dashboard
  • Phase 5: Beta testing with a group of pilot merchants, fixes and rollout

What we delivered

An omnichannel sales platform with separate pricing for consumers and B2B resellers, Orange Money and MTN MoMo payments, multi-warehouse management and a mobile app for delivery drivers.
